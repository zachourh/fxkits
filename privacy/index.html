Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information FXKits collects when you use fxkits.io, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and written it in plain language.

1. Who we are

FXKits is a collection of browser-based visual effect tools for designers and creators, available at fxkits.io. FXKits is operated by Zakaria Ourhou, who is responsible for the information described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, write to hi@zakariaourhou.com.

2. Information we collect

Your account

You need a free account to use the tools. You can sign in with Google or with an email address and a password.

If you sign up with email, we receive your email address, and we send you a link to confirm it. Your password is stored in protected form by our sign-in provider, and we cannot see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, your name and your profile picture from Google, and no password.

Usage information

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects information such as the pages you visit, the time you spend on them, the site that referred you, your approximate location based on your IP address, and your browser and device type.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic request data, such as your IP address, the date and time, and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

What we do not collect

The images, videos, fonts and artwork you load into an FXKits tool are processed in your own browser. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we never see, store or share them. The same is true of the files you export.

We do not collect payment details.

3. How we use your information

  • To give you access. Your account signs you in and unlocks the tools.
  • To email you. We send occasional emails about new tools, updates and related design work. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve FXKits. Usage information shows us which tools are used and where people run into problems.
  • To keep the site working and secure. Technical information helps us prevent abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on your consent to send you emails, and on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the site for the rest. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing.

5. Services we use

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run FXKits. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and stores your account details.
  • Kit (kit.com) stores your email address and first name, and sends our emails.
  • Google Analytics provides usage statistics.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and Dropbox deliver fonts, code libraries and demo media used by some tools. Your browser requests these files directly, which shares your IP address with those services.

6. Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visitors apart and measure usage.

We also keep your sign-in session in your browser's storage so that you stay signed in between visits, unless you turn off "Keep me signed in". You can end it at any time with the Sign out link on the homepage, or by clearing the site data for fxkits.io in your browser settings.

You can block cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on. The tools still work if you do.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it, and your email address on our mailing list until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics, after which it is deleted automatically. Hosting logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it,
  • ask us to correct or delete it,
  • object to or restrict how we use it,
  • withdraw your consent to emails at any time,
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email hi@zakariaourhou.com. We reply within 30 days. To stop our emails, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send.

9. International transfers

FXKits is available worldwide, and the providers listed above may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, those transfers are covered by the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

FXKits is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the site. No online service can guarantee complete security, but we keep the information we hold to a minimum to reduce the risk.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FXKits changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: hi@zakariaourhou.com.
